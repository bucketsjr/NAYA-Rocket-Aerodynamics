# Manual public-release and IP checklist

*Passing `scripts/check_release.py` does not grant IP permission or establish title.*

- [ ] Check NAYA/SEM400 course assessment agreements and all applicable Deakin student IP conditions and sponsorship terms.
- [ ] Obtain permission for joint work and third-party CAD, figures, photographs, data and code. Deakin states students usually own original work but exceptions apply to arrangements and agreements.
- [ ] Do not copy instructor-supplied templates, original coursework report or externally owned AIAA wing data without permission.
- [ ] Do not release work product, controlled technical data or proprietary information from any employer.
- [ ] Inspect media/metadata and any future logs for names, credentials, personal details and system identifiers.
- [ ] Keep *assumed concept geometry*, *new 2-D inviscid result* and *unrun 3-D RANS template* labels in captions.
- [ ] Confirm whether permitting commercial reuse under MIT + CC BY 4.0 matches your intentions.
- [ ] Review, select and substantively revise AI-assisted code, diagrams and descriptions, document your human contribution, and avoid assuming that an author name or licence guarantees Australian copyright.
- [ ] Confirm author's name, date and any acknowledged collaborators before publication.
- [ ] Verify that each result has an actual reproducible source and interpretation limits.
- [ ] Publish under a personal account or an entity entitled to license the files.

Deakin source: https://www.deakin.edu.au/students/research-support/policies-and-procedures/intellectual-property
